# Epic work

Read AGENTS.md, [the repository profile](../../../docs/development/repository-profile.md) and the epic's full acceptance/comments/child records. Load [issue work](../hvo-issue-work/SKILL.md), [PR lifecycle](../hvo-pr-lifecycle/SKILL.md), and route independent reviewers to [code review](../hvo-code-review/SKILL.md).

Claim the epic visibly with distinct session identity, revision, authorized child scope and final acceptance. Build a durable queue from actual dependencies and existing ownership. Closed child issues alone do not prove final acceptance.

For each dependency-ready authorized child, use its own claim and normally branch/PR. Preserve other owners' claims. Record child PRs, review/CI/merge results and blockers on the epic. Continue to the next ready authorized child after closeout without routine permission. If a child is blocked, pursue independent ready work and report the decision needed.

An assigned epic does not authorize unrelated features, deployment, release or every historical linked issue. Create/split children only when needed for the assigned scope and within its authorization. Sequential work is valid; no fleet registry, heartbeat, lease or multi-agent enrollment is required. Delegate only when authorized and supported, carrying scope/procedures and exact review source; preserve each session's provenance.

Verify final epic acceptance and required integration evidence after children deliver. Close only when dependencies and acceptance are satisfied, with final links/outcomes/follow-ups, released claims and safe cleanup. If the outcome cannot be established, leave the epic incomplete and provide a resumable handoff.
